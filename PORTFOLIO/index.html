WHAT DOES “CYBERPUNK” MEAN?

Born in the 1980s as a science-fiction subgenre, it fuses cyber (networks, machines, code) with punk (rebellion, the margins, refusal). It imagines a near future where high technology shares the street with decay, megacorporations and deep inequality.

Visually it is neon, rain, chrome implants and night cities that never sleep. Orbitron holds that tension: precise geometric construction with the soul of a future that never fully arrived. Series such as Cyberpunk: Edgerunners pushed the aesthetic back into mass culture.
